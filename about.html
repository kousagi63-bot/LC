Our Journey

Firm History

From a two-attorney partnership to a trusted legal services firm — our story is one of relentless growth and unwavering commitment.

1998
Founded in New York

James Stackly and Margaret Chen establish the firm with a vision to provide premium legal services with a personal touch.

2003
Family & Property Practice

Expanded our practice to include comprehensive family law and real estate services, serving a growing personal client base.

2008
Litigation Practice Launch

Formalized our litigation and dispute resolution practice, which quickly earned a reputation for proven courtroom results.

2012
Criminal Defense Expansion

Grew our criminal law team to provide strong, strategic defense representation for clients in serious criminal matters.

2018
50 Attorneys Milestone

Reached 50 attorneys firm-wide and recognized as a leading legal services firm for comprehensive practice coverage.

2024
Full-Service Leadership

Expanded across 10 practice areas, serving over 500 clients with 50+ specialized attorneys under one roof.
